Copy everything inside the code block below and paste it into Google Flow.

```text
COMMAND: Generate exactly one photorealistic lifestyle product advertisement.

STRICT TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS (non-negotiable):
- Output: square 1000×1000 pixels, PNG, clean edges, no border, no watermark.
- Style: realistic photography, natural soft daylight, minimal elegant home interior, premium lifestyle presentation.

- Maximum one person if required for scale or demonstration: shown only partially or from behind, face never visible, products remain dominant and fully clear.
- Forbidden: winter snow, excessive Christmas decorations, multiple faces, people looking at camera, cluttered background, competing objects, invented products, box shots, product packaging shots, store environments.

PRODUCT LIST (use ONLY these exact items from the reference image, nothing else):
[PASTE EXACT PRODUCT NAMES AND BRIEF VISUAL DESCRIPTIONS HERE]

Final instruction: Every element must obey the above constraints with zero deviation. Products first, lifestyle second, nothing else.
```
